        How to play

        Every day there is one new equation. Work out the value of x and enter it. You get 6 attempts, and x is always a whole number.

        7(x + 4) = 42  gives  x = 2

        Puzzles get harder through the week: easy on Monday, medium Tuesday and Wednesday, hard Thursday and Friday, expert at the weekend.

        When you finish, you'll see the worked solution. Solve on consecutive days to build a streak.

        A new puzzle goes live at midnight in your time zone. Missed one? Play it from the archive. Archive puzzles don't affect your streak or statistics.

        Practice gives you unlimited puzzles at any level. They don't count towards your statistics either.
