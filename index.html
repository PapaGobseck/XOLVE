          Keep it balanced

          An equation is like a set of scales. Whatever you do to one side, do exactly the same to the other, and it stays true.

          x + 4 = 10subtract 4 from both sidesx = 6

          Undo with the opposite

          To get x on its own, undo what's been done to it using the inverse operation.

          You seeDo this to both sides
          x + 5Subtract 5
          x − 5Add 5
          5xDivide by 5
          x5Multiply by 5

          Reading the notation

          5x means 5 × x. A number written in front of a bracket multiplies everything inside it, so 3(x + 2) means 3 × (x + 2). A fraction line means divide: x4 is x ÷ 4.

          Order of operations (BIDMAS)

          Calculations happen in this order: Brackets, Indices, Division and Multiplication, Addition and Subtraction. When solving, undo them in reverse: deal with adding and subtracting first, then multiplying and dividing, then brackets.

          2x + 3 = 11subtract 3 first, then divide by 2x = 4

          Brackets

          Either divide both sides by the number outside the bracket, or expand it by multiplying each term inside:

          a(b + c) = ab + acfor example3(x + 4) = 3x + 12

          A minus sign in front of a bracket changes every sign inside: −(x + 3) = −x − 3, and −2(x − 5) = −2x + 10.

          Square brackets are just brackets. They're used when one bracket sits inside another, and you work from the outside in.

          4[5(x − 3) + 8] = 212divide by 4 first5(x − 3) + 8 = 53

          Collecting like terms

          Terms with x combine with each other, and plain numbers combine with each other.

          4x + 7 + 2x = 6x + 7

          x on both sides

          Subtract the smaller x term from both sides, so x only appears once.

          5x + 2 = 3x + 10subtract 3x from both sides2x + 2 = 10

          Fractions

          Multiply both sides by the number on the bottom to clear the fraction. With more than one fraction, multiply by the lowest common denominator: the smallest number they all divide into.

          x + 32 = 5multiply both sides by 2x + 3 = 10
          x2 + x3 = 5multiply both sides by 63x + 2x = 30

          Negative numbers

          Two minuses multiplied or divided make a plus. Dividing a negative by a negative gives a positive answer.

          −3x = −12divide both sides by −3x = 4

          Check your answer

          Put your value of x back into the original equation. If both sides come out the same, you're right.

          3(x + 4) = 21 with x = 33 × 7 = 21✓

          How to play

          Every day there is one new equation. Work out the value of x and enter it. You get 6 attempts, and x is always a whole number.

          Puzzles get harder through the week: easy on Monday, medium Tuesday and Wednesday, hard Thursday and Friday, expert at the weekend.
